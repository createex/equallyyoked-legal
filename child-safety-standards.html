Child Safety Standards

Equally Yoked Adventist Dating app, published by JSTARKSTECH
Effective Date: November 3, 2026

Equally Yoked is an adults-only dating and friendship community. We have zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and for child sexual abuse material (CSAM). This page describes the standards we publish and follow.

1. Adults only

  • Users must be at least 18 years old to create an account. Date of birth is collected at sign-up and accounts for anyone under 18 are refused.
  • If we learn that a user is under 18, the account is removed and its data deleted.
  • The app is listed with an 18+ target audience and a mature content rating on app stores.

2. Prohibited content and conduct

The following are prohibited on Equally Yoked and result in immediate account removal and, where required, a report to the authorities:

  • Uploading, sharing, requesting, or describing any sexual content involving a minor.
  • Sexualizing, grooming, soliciting, or attempting to contact minors.
  • Misrepresenting one's age to gain access to the service, or presenting a minor on a profile.
  • Any content that depicts, promotes, or normalizes the sexual abuse or exploitation of children.

3. How to report a concern

Anyone, whether or not they use the app, can report a child safety concern:

  1. Email starkstechj@gmail.com with the subject line Child safety report. Include the profile name and any screenshots.
  2. Inside the app, use the Unmatch option in a conversation to immediately stop contact with the other person, then email us using the address above.

Reports are reviewed by a designated person within 24 hours. The reporter's identity is kept confidential.

4. What happens after a report

  • The reported account is suspended while we review.
  • Confirmed CSAE or CSAM leads to permanent removal of the account and preservation of the relevant evidence.
  • We report confirmed CSAM to the National Center for Missing and Exploited Children (NCMEC) through its CyberTipline, and cooperate with law enforcement in any jurisdiction as required by law.

5. Compliance

We comply with applicable child safety laws, including United States federal law on reporting child sexual abuse material (18 U.S.C. 2258A), and with the child safety policies of Google Play and the Apple App Store.
