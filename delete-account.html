Delete Your Account

Equally Yoked Adventist Dating app, published by JSTARKSTECH

You can request deletion of your Equally Yoked account and the personal data associated with it at any time.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to starkstechj@gmail.com from the email address registered on your account.
  2. Use the subject line Delete my Equally Yoked account.
  3. We will verify that the request came from the account owner and delete the account within 30 days. You will receive a confirmation email when it is done.

You can also find this instruction inside the app under the profile icon, then Settings, then Delete Account.

What is deleted

  • Your login credentials and account
  • Your profile, including name, date of birth, gender, city, church, bio, and questionnaire answers
  • Your profile photos
  • Your matches, likes, and passes
  • Your conversations and messages
  • Your subscription access records held by the app

What is kept, and for how long

  • Purchase records held by Google Play or the Apple App Store are managed by those stores under their own policies. Cancel any active subscription in your store account to stop renewals.
  • Encrypted database backups may contain your data for up to 30 days after deletion, after which they are overwritten.
  • Records we are required to keep by law, such as reports of abuse, may be retained for as long as the law requires.
